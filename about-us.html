Our Story

The Struggle for Scalable Tech Talent

Our journey in the startup world has taken us through the exciting (and sometimes messy) world of fashion tech, B2B ventures, and even building a seller service SaaS platform. Throughout these experiences, one thing remained constant: the critical need for talented tech resources.

We understand firsthand the challenges of building an in-house tech team. The recruitment process can be time-consuming, and finding the perfect fit with the required skill set and budget can feel like searching for a unicorn. Download this burden to us and we will get you the best talent immediately to suit your needs.

A Vision for Streamlined Tech Hiring

We've all been there, wishing there was a simpler way. That's why Mernplus was born. We saw a gap in the market for a service that could take the pain out of tech hiring. We wanted to create a solution that would allow businesses like ours to find reliable, skilled tech talent quickly and efficiently, all within budget.

Mernplus: Your Tech Talent Partner primarily in providing Developers for Hire

Mernplus leverages our experience in the tech startup space to connect you with the perfect tech resources. We understand the specific needs of businesses like yours, and are committed to finding the talent that aligns with your vision and goals. You get all technical support in ideation, design, and development at world-class standards from our highly committed team that will develop everything as our own.

Here's what sets us apart:

  • Effortless Recruitment: We handle the entire recruitment process, saving you valuable time and resources, and provide you with the resources as and when needed.
  • Immediate start: We maintain a talent pool that can start working on your thoughts in just a 2-day timeframe.
  • Best expertise and experience to leverage: It is not just the talent we provide but also the expertise and experience of our founders and leaders to give you a big knowledge bank to explore. We handhold all our developers in their initial client work to give a seamless experience to the client.
  • Proven Track Record: Our team has a deep understanding of the tech landscape, ensuring we find the best talent for your needs.
  • Cost-Effective Solutions: We work within your budget to find the perfect fit, without compromising on quality. Daily stand-up calls and Agile methodology for each module or scrums work out to be very productive in terms of getting the output from a remote team even for shorter durations.

Let Mernplus help you build your dream tech team. Contact us today and let's discuss your specific needs!
